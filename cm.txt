The firmware agent read both tables and RETRACTED FW-006's arithmetic. Per S9 a
retraction is a finding and gets the same treatment, so I validated it before

Their correction: AKM splits TDM128's eight channels across SDTI1-2, so it is
four channels per pin x 32 bits = 128 bit-times. 128 fs IS correct for TDM128,
and our two citations were correct as datasheet facts. Nothing was mistranscribed.

But their conclusion survives for a different reason, and the copper settles it:
gen_sheet_dac.py:206 grounds SDTI2-4 on both DACs. Mode 12 needs SDTI2, which
would carry pairs 3-4 - so AOUT5, AOUT6, AOUT11 and AOUT12 would receive constant
zero. Four of twelve outputs silent with nothing reporting it.

Resolution: ADC = Table 9 row 26 / TDM128 / 128 fs / 6.144 MHz. DAC = Table 13
Mode 18 / TDM256 / 256 fs / 12.288 MHz. TDM256 puts eight 32-bit slots on SDTI1
alone, which is exactly S4's TDM8 on the one wire the board has. NO SCHEMATIC
CHANGE - SDTI2/3/4 at GND is correct for TDM256 and matches DCHAIN=0.

And it was worse than 005 recorded: the bad figure was ONE wrong copy against
THREE right ones - CLAUDE.md S6, STATE.md T2-01, and REQUIREMENTS S4's port
table - with the wrong one in S0 LIVE STATUS, the block read at the start of
every session. A majority of correct copies did not help, because nothing ever
compared them.

FW-010 answered: the two links do NOT share a BICK rate, and checked on their
question - no series termination exists on any BCLK net in either generator and
layout has not started, so nothing was ever sized against 6.144 MHz and there is
nothing to unwind. Recorded for layout that BCLK is two net classes, not one.

FW-011 logged: SDS2-0 must be 000 on both DACs. Every other value reaches into
the grounded SDTI2 and silences channels. No hardware backstop, no error bit,
and the failure is PARTIAL - a board where AOUT5-6 are dead and AOUT1-4 are fine
is a very convincing analog fault. Filed with the matched-filter-modes invariant.

M35, from their diagnosis of why F70's check could not have caught F70: a
consistency check between two datasheets with no third input passes whenever the
parts are similar, and two parts from one vendor will almost always share some
compatible row. The check confirmed the parts CAN agree; it could not confirm
they agree at the settings this board uses, because it never consulted the board.
A compatibility check needs THREE inputs - both datasheets AND the artefact.
