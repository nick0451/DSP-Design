F97 proposal + three operator decrees: clock /2 locked, 85C parts, cabin noise

F97 - the precedent search answered it better than a number could. The three
missing words have a clear industry answer: 20Hz-20kHz AES17 brickwall
(unanimous), PEAK not RMS (TI and Audio Precision both state it outright),
silent input (unanimous).

But the load-bearing discovery is that BANDWIDTH WAS THE WRONG QUESTION. TI
own acceptance mask spans 10.715 mV at 100 Hz to 0.407 mV at 2.63 kHz - a 26.3x
spread, which is my factor-of-25 sitting inside a real vendor specification. TI
does not resolve it by picking a bandwidth; it uses a frequency-dependent MASK,
and the modern datasheets collapse that mask into one number with a WEIGHTING
FILTER. A 10 mV LF thump and a 0.4 mV midband tick are equally audible.

The weighting is not arbitrary. ITU-R BS.468 quasi-peak meter de-weights a 1 ms
burst by 13.5 dB and follows ~9 dB/decade, which is the ear 3 dB per halving
law (Moore, Springer Handbook 13.1). The meter was built to imitate temporal
integration.

SECOND FINDING the search did not reach for: we measure in the wrong place.
Every industry pop number is at the SPEAKER; S12 measures at our RCA output,
before an amplifier whose gain we do not control. Our 10 mV referred forward is
4.5x to 28.5x LOOSER than TI typ. S12 10 mV looks conservative and is not.

No value proposed, deliberately. F99 still comes first.

DECREES:

D-1 clock: the /2 architecture is locked in. 49.152 MHz at ordinary 45/55,
divided on board. It also solves the temperature half for free - the 48/52
constraint was what forced SiT7601 (no stock); with a divider the whole 45/55
catalogue reopens, including SiT1618 at -40/+125. REQUIREMENTS S3 is [HARD] and
names 24.576 MHz - that edit is the operator act, not ours. Divider part NOT
chosen and will not be chosen from memory: whether a single-gate flip-flop
exposes Q-bar is a pin-table question.

D-2 temperature: not direct sun, cabin under 160F (71.1C), parts rated 85C.
Supersedes my +85C-ambient recommendation - the operator has better information
about the actual install than my literature figure. Settles both AKM pads as
GROUNDED: tab-open on the AK5574 is -40..+70C, below the decreed cabin, so it
fails on the operator own number. But self-heating is now the binding term:
71.1C against an 85C rating leaves 13.9C for enclosure rise, and a 12W board in
a sealed case exceeds it. That is Block E, UNSET, and now load-bearing on a
parts decision - recorded at both ends per S8.11.

D-3 cabin noise 60-80 dB, consistent with the measured data. One caveat that
cuts the other way: power-down happens at ignition-off, engine stopped, fan off,
which is the quietest moment the car has at 42-49 dB(A). The pop we specify
against is heard with 20-30 dB less masking than the driving figure implies.
