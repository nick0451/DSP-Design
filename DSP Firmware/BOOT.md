# BOOT.md — ODSP-12 turn-on and turn-off specification

**Firmware implementation spec for `REQUIREMENTS.md` §7.2 [HARD] and §7.3 [HARD] (v1.8, with the
2026-08-22 step-swap decree).**

Scope: the ESP32-S3's sequencing of the ADAU1466, AK5574 and two AK4458s. Governed by
`../CLAUDE.md`; evidence tiers per §5. **Every register value here was read from the datasheet page
cited, rendered as a page image — none from memory.** Findings and open items are tracked in
[`STATE.md`](STATE.md).

**Status: SPECIFICATION ONLY. No code exists — the ESP-IDF toolchain is not installed (T1-F05) and
the version pin is still ⬜ UNSET (Q2a).**

---

## 0. The three timing windows — they are distinct and must not be merged

| # | Window | Bus | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | **PLL lock, ≤ 15 ms** | SPI0 | The ADAU1466's slave control ports are **inactive until the PLL locks**; every write is silently discarded. Worst case 10.666 ms at 48 kHz, typically ~3.5 ms |
| **W2** | **ADC wake-up, ≥ 12.5 ms** | **I2C0, bus-wide** | The AK5574 may emit instantaneous pulses (**max 1 µs**) on its digital pins. AKM directs that traffic with *any* device on the same bus be avoided |
| **W3** | **Reset around a format change** | I2C0, one device | Changing `CKS`/`TDM`/`DIF` on the AK5574 requires the part be **in** reset; a stable clock must be supplied after release |

**W1 and W2 are on different buses with different causes and different durations. W3 is a motion,
not a delay.** Merging any two of them produces a boot that works on the bench and fails at
temperature.

## 1. Turn-on — `REQUIREMENTS.md` §7.2 [HARD]

### 1.1 Preconditions satisfied by hardware, not by firmware

**MCLK free-runs.** §7.2 step 5a-pre: the clock tree runs from `+3V3_CLK` off `+6V2_A`, up before
the MCU finishes booting, with the fanout's `1G` straps high. **The requirements deliberately did
not create a firmware step for this, because a step that can be misordered is a step that will
be.** Verified at bring-up (`BRINGUP.md`), not sequenced here.

> ⚠ **BICK and LRCK are NOT in that category — see §1.3 and FW-016.**

### 1.2 Sequence

    1.  Remote-in asserted (or signal-sense) -> protected rails enable
    2.  Digital rails up -> ESP32 boots
    3.  ESP32 releases DSP reset
    3a. PLL: write 0xF000, 0xF001, 0xF002, 0xF003
        then POLL 0xF004 bit 0 (PLL_LOCK) until set, 15 ms timeout        [W1]
        *** TIMEOUT IS A DISTINCT FAULT CODE, NOT A RETRY LOOP ***
        A PLL that will not lock means the XO or its supply is broken.
        Retried writes repair nothing and hide the real fault.
    3b. Power up and configure the clock generators and serial ports so that
        BICK and LRCK are RUNNING before step 5a.  See S1.4.               [FW-016]
    4.  Slave-boot the program over SPI; verify readback via 0xF405 CORE_STATUS
    5.  Converter configuration phase:
        5a. Release AK5574 PDN (GPIO9)                                     [W2 starts]
        5b. HOLD ALL I2C0 TRAFFIC for >= 12.5 ms -- bus-wide, not just the ADC
            Interleave with step 4: the SPI program load is bus-independent
            and takes longer than 12.5 ms anyway, so the wait costs nothing.
        5c. Configure ADC and both DACs in one pass, DACs held in soft-mute
    6.  Analog rails confirmed in-window (ADS1015) -> release output mute,
        DAC soft-unmute ramp
    7.  Assert remote-out after a configurable delay (default 500 ms post-unmute)

### 1.3 ⚠ Step 3b is not in `REQUIREMENTS.md` §7.2 — see FW-016

**§7.2's step 5b holds the bus for `max(10 ms lockout, 583/fs ≈ 12.1 ms)`.**

> **`583/fs` is a count of LRCK periods. If LRCK is not running when `PDN` is released, that term
> does not elapse — it does not start.** A firmware that waits 12.5 ms of wall-clock and then
> proceeds will find an ADC that never initialised, and `SDTO` never becomes valid.

**`MCLK` free-runs (§1.1). `BICK` and `LRCK` do not — they are generated by the ADAU1466's serial
port**, which is dead until the PLL locks (W1) and its clock generators and serial ports are
configured. **Step 3b exists to close that gap.** Raised as **FW-016**, concurred by hw and
**escalated to the operator**; the §7.2 text is the operator's to amend.

### 1.4 Step 3b in detail — and the clock generators are POWERED DOWN at reset

**T1, `adau1462-1466.pdf` Rev. C p.31:** the three clock generators are fed from the **PLL output**
(nominally 294.912 MHz) and configured by `N`/`M` control registers. Each of the 15 resulting clock
domains produces the LRCLK and BCLK for the serial ports, with **BCLK available at 32, 64, 128, 256
and 512 BCLK/sample**. p.57: the serial ports are **functional blocks** configured entirely by
`SERIAL_BYTE_x_0/_1`, including **master or slave operation**.

> **So BICK/LRCK generation is a control-register affair driven by the PLL — it does not require the
> DSP core to be executing.** That is what makes step 3b placeable before the program load, and it
> preserves §7.2 5c's interleaving argument intact.

⚠ **But they do not start on their own.** `0xF050` `POWER_ENABLE0` carries `CLK_GEN3_PWR`,
`CLK_GEN2_PWR`, `CLK_GEN1_PWR`, `SOUT3_PWR`…`SOUT0_PWR`, `SIN3_PWR`…`SIN0_PWR` — **reset `0x0000`.**
**The clock generators and every serial port are powered down out of reset and must be explicitly
enabled.**

    3b-i.   0xF001 PLL_CTRL1 = 0x0003   prescale DIVIDE BY 8
                                        Our MCLK is 24.576 MHz [HARD]; p.98 requires the PLL
                                        input be as close to 3.072 MHz as possible.
                                        24.576 / 8 = 3.072 MHz exactly.
                                        (The datasheet's own worked example uses 12.288 MHz
                                        and divide-by-4 -- do not copy it.)
            0xF000 PLL_CTRL0 = 0x0060   feedback divider 96, THE DEFAULT
                                        3.072 MHz x 96 = 294.912 MHz system clock
            0xF002, 0xF003              clock source select, then PLL enable
            poll 0xF004 bit 0                                              [W1]
    3b-ii.  0xF050 POWER_ENABLE0        enable CLK_GEN1, SOUT0, SOUT1, SIN0
                                        *** reset is 0x0000 -- nothing runs until this write ***
    3b-iii. 0xF020-0xF027 CLK_GEN1      N=1, M=6 are the DEFAULTS and give exactly 48 kHz:
                                        294.912e6 / (1024 x 6) = 48000.000 Hz
    3b-iv.  0xF200-0xF21D SERIAL_BYTE_* master mode, 32-bit, MSB-justified, LRCLK rising:
                                        SIN0  (ADC)  BCLK = 128 fs =  6.144 MHz
                                        SOUT0 (DAC A) BCLK = 256 fs = 12.288 MHz
                                        SOUT1 (DAC B) BCLK = 256 fs = 12.288 MHz
                                        Both rates are in the generator's 32/64/128/256/512 set,
                                        so FW-010's two BCLK classes are directly producible.

**Every number above is arithmetic you can check, not a recalled value.** `294.912e6/(1024×6)` and
`24.576/8` are both exact.

## 2. The boot register writes — ALL are masked writes

> **THE RULE (FD-12): on both AKM parts, every register the boot path writes is a
> read-modify-write, because not one of them contains only the field being set.** The boot code
> writes **fields, not values**. A helper taking `(register, value)` rather than
> `(register, mask, value)` is a defect waiting for its first use — and the failure is silent, with
> no fault and no error bit.

### 2.1 AK5574 (`0x10`) — wrong on three counts at every `PDN` cycle

All registers reset to defaults whenever `PDN` goes low (Note 26, p.62). **W3 applies: the part must
be in reset while `CKS`/`TDM`/`DIF` change.**

    write(01H, 0x00)    RSTN = 0     enter reset      [W3]  MONO2-1 preserved at 00 (I-1)
    write(02H, 0x35)    CKS3-0=0110  512 fs / Normal Speed   was 0000 = 128 fs / Quad / 108-216 kHz
                        DIF1-0=10    32-bit MSB              was 00   = 24-bit MSB
                        HPFE  =1     PRESERVED (FD-11)       naive 0x34 would clear it
    write(03H, 0x20)    TDM1-0=01    TDM128 = 4 slots        was 00   = Normal / non-TDM
                                     the only register here with no other live bits
    write(01H, 0x01)    RSTN = 1     release reset; stable clock must already be present
    -- 04H needs NO write: default 00H is Sharp roll-off / Normal delay / PCM, all correct

### 2.2 AK4458 ×2 (`0x11`, `0x12`) — identical writes to both

    write(00H, 0x0D)    RSTN=1 AND DIF2-0=110 PRESERVED
                        *** naive 0x01 clears DIF2-0 to 000, which Table 13 SHADES as
                            "not available" in TDM256 -- the part is driven to a
                            configuration the vendor declines to characterise ***
    write(0AH, 0x8D)    TDM1-0=10 (TDM256 = 8 slots on SDTI1)
                        PW2=PW1=1 PRESERVED, DEM21-0=01 (OFF) PRESERVED, SDS bits untouched
                        *** naive 0x80 powers OFF DAC1 and DAC2 -- four of eight channels --
                            and sets DEM21-0=00 = 44.1 kHz de-emphasis on a 48 kHz board,
                            about 9.5 dB of loss at 20 kHz on DAC2 (AK4458 p.48 Table 17) ***
    -- 07H: DO NOT WRITE. Default 0x03; SYNCE already 1 and D1 is a fixed 1 that a
       naive 0x01 would clear. If ever written, read-modify-write.
    -- 05H, 06H, 08H, 09H, 0BH need NO write.

**`DIF2-0` must be re-verified AFTER the `0AH` TDM write (FW-004).** `110` is Mode 6 in Normal mode
and Mode 18 in TDM256 — **both 32-bit MSB justified, so it does survive here** — but that is a fact
about this value, not a property of the field. **A readback that sees `110` passes in both the
correct and the incorrect case.**

### 2.3 ADAU1466

    0xF690  SPDIF_TX_EN    TXEN = 1    the on-die S/PDIF transmitter is DISABLED at reset,
                                       so nothing leaves SPDIFOUT until firmware writes it
    0xF784-0xF787 / 0xF794-0xF797      pin drive and slew for the serial outputs.
                                       There is NO series termination on any BCLK net, so
                                       edge rate on the two 12.288 MHz DAC clocks is a
                                       FIRMWARE choice. Leaving the reset defaults is a
                                       decision; record it as one. (FW-013)

## 3. The invariant shelf — correct by default, protected by not writing

**No hardware backs any of these. No error bit reports any of them. Each is silent when violated.
Today the code does nothing and doing nothing is correct — which is exactly why they cannot be
found by reading the code, and must exist as a list.**

| # | Invariant | Protects | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-1 | AK5574 `MONO2-1` = `00` (Not-Summation) | §4/§10 [HARD] slot map | p.62 |
| I-2 | AK5574 `PW4-1` = `1111` (all channels on) | §4/§10 [HARD] slot map | p.62 |
| I-3 | AK4458 `SDS2-0` = `000` | the grounded `SDTI2` | p.80 |
| I-4 | AK4458 `SYNCE` = `1` | §5 item 5 alignment | p.79 |
| I-5 | AK4458 `07H` = `0x03` (D1 fixed `1`) | a naive `0x01` clears it | p.79 |
| I-6 | AK4458 `DCHAIN` = `0` | the two-port topology | p.43 |
| I-7 | Both AK4458 filter modes identical | §5 item 5 group delay | §5 contract |
| **I-8** | **AK4458 `FIR2-0` (`0CH` D2:D0) = `000`** — the default | **the 115 dB spec itself** | p.50 Table 20 |

**I-8 is the odd one and deserves its own note.** AK4458 p.50, verbatim: *"Best performance should be
achieved by clean linear regulated AVDD/VREF power supplies **and the default setting of the FIR2-0
bits**. **These conditions are included in the Analog Characteristics specs.**"*

> **The 115 dB figure is specified under TWO simultaneous conditions, and only one of them lives on
> a sheet where it can be changed.** `U6` (LT3045) feeds AVDD/VREF on both DACs. *"Replace `U6` with
> a buck"* is an ordinary cost suggestion — cheaper, more efficient — and it **invalidates the
> specification condition and turns a defaulted register three sheets away into a live decision**,
> because AKM's non-default `FIR` modes exist precisely to claw performance back when the rail is a
> switcher (the same page: the filter *"may reduce degradation of the analog characteristics caused
> by a switching regulator"*).

> ⚠ **So `FIR2-0` is reopened by a POWER-SUPPLY change, not by anything in the audio path.** That is
> the only entry on this shelf whose trigger is outside firmware's own domain.

**Why I-1 and I-2 matter:** in serial control mode the AK5574 is permanently in **Optimal Data
Placement** regardless of the `ODP` strap (p.46). Optimal placement **removes summation
redundancy**, so the number of occupied slots is a function of the summation setting. §4's TDM map
and §10's channel↔slot naming are [HARD] and cannot follow. **Powering down an unused ADC channel or
summing two inputs for 3 dB of S/N both read as free wins and both silently re-pack every slot.**

⚠ **I-3 is NOT protected by "do not write" — `SDS1`/`SDS2` live in `0AH`, which the boot path
writes. It is protected by writing `0x8D` rather than `0x80`.** The shelf and the boot-write table
are one invariant seen from two sides.

## 4. Turn-off — §7.3 [HARD], as amended 2026-08-22

**Operator decree: steps 2 and 3 swapped.** The original order engaged the hard mute on live
programme — a **6.36 V** step at full-scale peak against §12's **0.2 mV** weighted-peak limit,
**90 dB over**. No component closes that; the swap stops the step existing rather than attenuating
it, and it removes the dependency on a third-party amplifier's unspecified mute latency.

    1. Remote-in drop detected -> remote-out released immediately
    2. State persisted, DACs SOFT-MUTED
    3. Hard mute engaged, into digital silence      <- must NOT begin until 2 COMPLETES
    4. Rails allowed to fall

### 4.1 The wait in step 2 is elapsed-time, and it is forced

**There is no ramp-complete status bit on the AK4458.** The only related output is `DZF`, which is
input-data zero-detect over **8192 LRCK = 170.7 ms** — a different and slower event — and **the
`DZF` pin is unconnected on both parts anyway.**

    ramp_ms = ATT_STEPS[ATS] / fs        ATT_STEPS = {4080, 2040, 510, 255}
    wait    = ramp_ms x margin           unconditional, before releasing MUTE_EN

**No hard-coded `85`.** At `ATS`=`00` (default) and 48 kHz this is **85.0 ms**.

⚠ **The datasheet states this two ways.** p.49 gives `4080/fs` and checks itself against its own
worked example (92.5 ms at 44.1 kHz); p.61 note (1) says *"4080 LRCK cycles (1020/fs)"*, which
contradicts itself inside one parenthesis — 21.25 ms. **Taking 85.0 ms**, on two independent grounds
that agree: p.49 is corroborated, and **the safe direction points the same way** — waiting too long
at shutdown is imperceptible, waiting too short reopens the 6.36 V step the swap was made to remove.

**The ramp is also not a constant:** it scales with `fs`, with `ATS1-0` (a 16:1 range), and with the
current `ATT_DATA` (4080 LRCK is the figure at full `FFH`→`00H` excursion). **That is why it is a
parameter.**

### 4.2 §7.4 is not firmware's

**De-energised = muted, by construction.** The disorderly path — crank sag, blown fuse, pulled
connector — has **no MCU participation by design**. **If you find yourself writing code that runs
during a rail collapse, something has been misread.**

## 5. Runtime behaviours this spec commits to

- **`SPDIF_LOSS_OF_LOCK` (`0xF605`) needs a runtime consumer, not a test hook** — it is the
  input-switching debounce signal and is exposed through the BLE/WS control API from day one
  (§5 contract item 4).
- **Non-PCM gating (FW-014).** `0xF602` `SPDIF_RX_DECODE` reports `AUDIO_TYPE`/`COMPR_TYPE`. S/PDIF
  can carry a compressed bitstream, and **routed to the DACs as PCM that is full-scale wideband
  noise into twelve channels — while `SPDIF_LOCK_DET` reads perfectly healthy.** **Lock is not the
  same as safe to unmute.** Input switching gates on `AUDIO_TYPE` as well as lock and will not
  unmute a non-PCM stream.
- **No health-check polling for the AK5574's autonomous phase-detect self-reset (F65).** It is
  unbounded, unobservable, and has no status bit; the AK5574 has **no status register at all**, and
  the only converter status output on the board is `OVF` on GPIO12, which reports overflow.
  **A guard that cannot observe its target is not a guard** (FD-09).

## 6. Firmware-owned GPIO

    GPIO9   ADC_PDN_N     AK5574 PDN
    GPIO10  DAC1_PDN_N    AK4458 #1 PDN
    GPIO11  DAC2_PDN_N    AK4458 #2 PDN
    GPIO12  ADC_OVF       AK5574 overflow (input)
    GPIO19/20             USB D+/D- -- RESERVED [HARD], never reassign

**All three `PDN` lines are independently firmware-owned**, which is what makes §1.2's ordering
implementable at all.
