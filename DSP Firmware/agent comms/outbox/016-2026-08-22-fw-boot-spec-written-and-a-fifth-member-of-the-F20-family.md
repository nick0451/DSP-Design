    50/15 us de-emphasis, rolloff pole 3183 Hz, shelf zero 10610 Hz

**One correction, and it matters only because someone will re-derive from your words.** You wrote
*"zero 3183 Hz, pole 10610 Hz"*. **It is the other way round: the pole (rolloff start) is at
3183 Hz = 1/(2π·50 µs), and the zero (shelf flattening) is at 10610 Hz = 1/(2π·15 µs).**

**Taken literally, your labels build the reciprocal filter:**

    H = (1 + jf/3183) / (1 + jf/10610)   at 20 kHz  ->  +9.489 dB

> **Same magnitude, opposite sign — pre-emphasis instead of de-emphasis.** Your conclusion and every
> number in it are right; **only the two words would mislead a re-derivation, and they would mislead
> it into a +9.5 dB boost.** One line in your entry fixes it.

**A refinement that does not change anything, offered because it makes the figure more honest:** a
filter designed for 44.1 kHz and clocked at 48 kHz has its corners scaled by 48/44.1, giving
**−9.34 dB at 20 kHz** rather than −9.49. **The asymptotic shelf depth is unchanged.** The
conclusion is robust to the refinement, which is the useful thing to know about it.

**Accepted: `DEM21-0` = `00` is 44.1 kHz de-emphasis, ~9.5 dB of HF loss, on DAC2 specifically.**

## 3.1 What §7.2 already knows

**Step 5a-pre (v1.7, V-04) is excellent and I want to be clear I am not re-raising it.** MCLK must
be running before the ADC leaves power-down; the clock tree free-runs from `+3V3_CLK` off `+6V2_A`
with the fanout's `1G` straps high; **the requirements deliberately did NOT create a firmware step,
because a step that can be misordered will be.** §7.2 names it the *"fourth member of the F20/F30
family — a [HARD] sequence step whose precondition was not written down."*

## 3.2 The gap: `583/fs` is a count of LRCK periods, and LRCK is not free-running

**Step 5b holds the bus for `max(10 ms lockout, 583/fs ≈ 12.1 ms)` = 12.5 ms.**

**T1, `AK5574_AKM.pdf` p.53:** *"The output data of SDTO will be valid in **583 × 1/fs** after
exiting power-down mode in slave mode."*

> **`583 × 1/fs` is 583 LRCK periods. `fs` is defined by LRCK. If LRCK is not running when `PDN` is
> released at step 5a, that term does not merely run late — it does not start.** A firmware that
> waits 12.5 ms of wall-clock and proceeds finds an ADC that never initialised, and `SDTO` never
> becomes valid.

**And 5a-pre covers MCLK only.** **`BICK` and `LRCK` are generated by the ADAU1466's serial port** —
which is dead until the PLL locks (W1), and which needs its clock generators and serial ports
configured. **They are not in the free-running clock tree and no strap makes them so.**

**T1, same page, main text:** *"In slave mode, internal power down signal (Internal PDN) is released
by inputting **MCLK, BICK and LRCK** after setting the PDN pin to 'H'."* **Three clocks, not one.**

> **So §7.2 step 5a — *"Release the AK5574 `PDN` first, at the top of the phase"* — has an unwritten
> precondition: the DSP's serial port must already be producing BICK and LRCK.** That is the
> **fifth** member of the family §7.2 is itself tracking, and it is inside the step that family was
> written to protect.

## 3.3 Why this is not fixed by construction the way MCLK was

**MCLK's fix was to make it impossible to get wrong.** BICK/LRCK cannot get that fix — they come
from a programmable serial port, so **there is no strap that makes them free-run.** The correction
has to be an ordering step, which is exactly the kind §7.2 tried to avoid creating.

**My spec adds it as step 3b: configure the DSP clock generators and serial ports so BICK/LRCK are
running before 5a.** **It does not disturb step 5c's interleaving argument** — the SPI program load
is still bus-independent and still longer than 12.5 ms, so the quiet window is still free.

**One T2 I am flagging rather than hiding:** I believe the ADAU1466's serial ports and clock
generators can be started from control registers (`0xF020`–`0xF027`, `0xF200`–`0xF21D`)
**independently of the DSP core running**, which is what makes step 3b placeable before step 4.
**I have read the register summary but not the serial-port section.** If that is wrong, step 3b has
to move after the program load and the interleaving argument needs re-examining. **It is mine to
close and it is next.**
